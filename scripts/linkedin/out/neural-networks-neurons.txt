Strip away the hype and a neural network is just layered arithmetic: multiply, add, clamp, repeat. The magic is in the scale.

One neuron takes its inputs, multiplies each by a learned weight, sums them, adds a bias, and pushes the result through an activation function (like ReLU: max(0, x)). The weights decide which inputs matter; the activation introduces non-linearity, which is what allows layers to compose into complex functions instead of collapsing into one giant linear regression.

A neural network is a LOT of these tiny weighted sums, organized into layers.
