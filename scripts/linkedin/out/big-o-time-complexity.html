Big O in 5 Minutes

How to predict your code's speed before the data grows.
1
O(1): constant time. Fast at any size — like dict lookups.
2
O(n): linear. 10x the data means 10x the work.
3
O(n log n): the sweet spot for sorting — merge sort lives here.
4
O(n²): nested loops. Fine for small n, painful at scale.
Worth rememberingNested loops are a smell. Ask: can I do this in one pass?
