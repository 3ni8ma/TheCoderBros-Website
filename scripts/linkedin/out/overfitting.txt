A model that scores 99% in training and 71% on new data did not learn your pattern — it memorized your dataset.

Underfitting happens when the model is too simple to capture the relationship (a line fit to a curve). Overfitting happens when it is flexible enough to memorize noise (a 50-degree polynomial that threads every point). The second is sneaky because training error looks amazing.

Performance on training data means nothing. Performance on unseen data means everything.
