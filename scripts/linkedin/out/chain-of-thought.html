Chain-of-Thought Prompting

The 2-line trick that makes LLMs dramatically more reliable at reasoning.
1
Ask the model to think step by step before answering.
2
Works because it mirrors how the model was trained to reason.
3
Zero-shot CoT: just add 'Let's think step by step.'
4
Use it for math, logic, debugging, and planning tasks.
text
Without CoT:
Q: A store has 3 shelves with 12 items each.
   20 items sell. How many remain?
A: 16   <- often wrong
 
With CoT:
A: First, total items: 3 x 12 = 36.
   Then subtract sold: 36 - 20 = 16.
   Answer: 16   <- reliable
Worth rememberingYou get better answers by asking for the reasoning, not just the answer.
