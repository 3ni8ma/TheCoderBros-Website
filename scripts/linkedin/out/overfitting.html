Overfitting & Underfitting

A model that memorizes vs a model that never learns.
1
Underfit: too simple — misses patterns, high error everywhere.
2
Overfit: too complex — perfect on training, fails on new data.
3
Validation set = your honest scoreboard.
4
Fix: more data, regularization, simpler model, early stopping.
